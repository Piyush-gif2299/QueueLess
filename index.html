About QueueLess

QueueLess is a smart digital queue management system designed to reduce waiting time, crowding and confusion.

🎟️

Digital Token

Users get a unique digital token instead of standing physically in a long queue.

⏱️

Waiting Time

The system estimates waiting time according to the number of people waiting in the queue.

👥

Crowd Status

Users can see whether the current crowd level is Low, Medium or High.

🔔

Alerts

Users can be notified when their turn is near, reducing unnecessary waiting.
